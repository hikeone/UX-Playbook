# Framing

This high-level format helps identify the single process within a department where you should begin applying AI. It’s intended for decision-makers, such as the department head and a mid-level manager within the department.

**Duration:** 4 hours.

**Participants:** Decision-makers — for example, the head of the department and a mid-level team member who is familiar with all relevant processes.

## 1. Mapping the department

**Map the department by capturing its overall tasks**

Take a look at the “Example departments mapped” where we mapped many standard departments.

Use the “Department Map Template” to map your department. This PDF can be edited using Adobe Acrobat. But we also added the InDesign file to edit the map. Align with the team, then print it with 90 cm height.

## 2. Collaboration Board

**Print the Collaboration Board**

**This printable PDF is four single A0-format page, serving as the Collaboration Board for the Framing session. Print it in A0 format.**

**Room preparation:**\
The Collaboration Board consists of two long sheets of paper, each about 5.5 meters long and 90 cm high—this represents the mapped department. Place each sheet on two long tables. Position the tables side by side so the sheets form one continuous surface, ensuring participants can access the Canvas from all sides.

In addition to your mapped department, use this Collaboration Board to guide the team through the Framing session.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (59).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## 3. Get the slides

Use these slides to kick off the Framing session and guide the team through the well-defined steps, including an introduction to the AI categories with example use cases. Each task has a dedicated slide with a built-in timer.

Feel free to adapt the slides to your corporate identity, and if you already have relevant AI use cases, replace the examples with your own.
