# Concept development

#### Concept Development & Assessment

Before this session, the process for automation has already been identified. In this session, the full client team or in-house team will develop an AI solution concept for that specific workflow.

**Duration:** 8 hours.

**Participants:** A decision-maker, individuals currently performing the process, an IT representative, and other relevant stakeholders depending on the process.

To get ready, there are three things you need to do:

## 1. Map the process

Here is a template for mapping the process. Focus on capturing the current state of the process or workflow — not on solutions or how AI might be applied. This is about visualizing the status quo as it exists today.

Start by creating a first draft using the template. Then share it with the client team or your internal team to refine it so it reflects reality. If the process is already documented, adapt it into our simplified, human-centered format so that anyone in the session can immediately engage with it.

The template is a PDF that can be edited using Adobe Acrobat. Once you've mapped out your process, print it and place it as the starting point on the Collaboration Board (see no. 2).
