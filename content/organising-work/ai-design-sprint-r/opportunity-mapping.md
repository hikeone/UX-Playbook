# Opportunity Mapping

This is the highest-level format designed to identify the single most relevant process within an organization or major business area where AI adoption should begin or should be next. It is intended for the leadership team of that organization or area.

**Duration:** 4–6 hours.

**Participants:** Leadership team and transformation leads—people whose roles require a view of the entire organization.

## 1. Mapping the organisation

**Prepare the “org chart”, or “value stream”, or “mapped business areas” in the correct format for use in the Opportunity Mapping session**

Determine which format the client uses to view their area from a high-level perspective—such as an “org chart”, a “value stream”, or “mapped business areas”. Whatever the format, use our templates to convert it into a structure we can work with during the Opportunity Mapping session.

## 2. Collaboration Board

#### Print the collaboration board

Use the Opportunity Mapping Collaboration Board alongside your org chart, value stream, or mapped business areas. The Collaboration Board guides your team through the Opportunity Mapping Session.

Simply download the printable PDF and have it printed in black/white on four A0 pages at your local print shop.

For the setup, use one long table for the Opportunity Mapping Collaboration Board (to fit the four A0 pages), and additional long tables for the “org chart”, “value stream”, or “mapped business areas”.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (56).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### Print the Summary Box

Use the Summary Box to prioritize AI opportunities alongside the Collaboration Board. Simply print it in black/white on A4 paper—print 30 copies to ensure you have enough for the session.

## 3. Get the slides

Use these slides to kick off the Opportunity Mapping session and guide the team through the well-defined steps, including an introduction to the AI categories with example use cases. Each task has a dedicated slide with a built-in timer.

Feel free to adapt the slides to your corporate identity, and if you already have relevant AI use cases, replace the examples with your own.
